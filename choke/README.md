# 1:1 Choke Balun

This is a fairly simple design for a box for a BalUn, designed to run along your coax line.

Parts:
- 3D Printed Box
- FT140-43 Toroidal Core
- RG316 coax cable (I don't have an exact length but probably ~20-30cm)
- 2 x BNC Bulkhead Connectors (Specifically designed around the plastic threaded D-version that I found on AliExpress for cheap)
- 10cm Wire (I used some 20AWG copper wire, but as long as it's rated for the power you're putting through it anything works)
- 2 x medium zip-ties

## Construction

### Step 1. Print the Box

I printed on a prusa MK3S with a .4mm nozzle in PLA filament. PETG may be more advisable if you're looking to install this outdoors or are using higher powers.

### Step 2. Wind the coax through the toroid

Aim for 8.5 turns on one side, 8.5 turns on the other, and leave yourself a centimeter or two for stripping and soldering to.

### Step 3. Insert the coax into the box

Put the coax into the printed box, use the zip-ties to zip-tie it in place, making sure to orient the bulk towards the center or sides, and not towards the lid.

Cut the coax to length and strip the ends, giving yourself enough room to solder to.

### Step 4. Soldering

Split your wire into 4 roughly equal parts, strip, and tin the ends with solder. Tin the ends of the coax as well.

Bend the ground pin of the bulkheads to roughly 90 degrees, and solder one end of each of your wires to each pin of the BNC bulkheads. All 4 of your wires should now have one end soldered.

### Step 5. Insert the Bulkhead BNC connectors into the case

You'll need to run the wires through the hole first, be sure to have them facing upwards.

### Step 6. Soldering

Solder each of the wires to their respective ends of the coax. The shield goes to the ground pin, the center to the signal pin.

### Step 7. Fit into lid and close

You'll want to push the wiring into the gaps on either side of the bulkhead connectors, and fit the lid on top. 

From there, you should be able to press fit the lid into the case. After the lid is fit, you may wish to use tape or plastic welds to stop it form coming out.
